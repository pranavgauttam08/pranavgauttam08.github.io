I'm a software engineer. I studied computer science and engineering at Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology in Patiala.

I got interested in AI-search visibility because it looks like a marketing problem and behaves like an engineering one. Identifiers have to stay stable. Markup has to agree with the page it describes. Crawlers have to be let in, and you need a way to check all of that that does not rely on trust. This site is where I work those things out in public.

## What I'm looking for

Roles where structured data, crawlability and AI-search visibility are treated as engineering work: schema generated from templates and data, validated in CI, and measured with logs and search tooling rather than guessed at.
